#### Featured: Heatwave Intelligence

A portal for NDMA that shows how heat in Pakistan has changed since 1980, and which districts face a heatwave in the next seven days.

[![Heatwave Intelligence: hot days per year across Pakistan, 1980 to 2024](heatwave-intelligence.webp)](https://hasnain061-ai.github.io/#work)

- 45 years of PMD station data (1980–2024) on a 0.45° grid, five heatwave measures at four percentile thresholds
- Swipe, side-by-side and difference comparison of any two periods or years
- Mann-Kendall trends, rankings, all-time records, and a chart for any place
- 7-day outlook from ECMWF, bias-corrected to 111 PMD stations, with Alert and Severe districts
- A chat assistant in English or Roman Urdu: every number comes from a data tool, and the answer is drawn on the map
